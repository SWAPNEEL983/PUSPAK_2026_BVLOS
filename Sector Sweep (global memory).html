Sector sweep

T sweep + hold POIs (MST network): the T sweeps back and forth. When a line drone finds a POI it reports it and stays on it; one of two reserve drones trailing the line takes its slot, and a new reserve is sent in. Each holder is linked to the nearest node of the permanent network (sentinel, trunk, other holders and their relays) with relays at most 100 m apart, so the network grows like a minimum spanning tree. The mission ends when every POI is reported, held, and linked.

POIs appear over time: the 10 POIs appear at random times within the window set by the slider (0 = all present from the start) and stay once they appear. A seen cell can now gain a POI later, so the adaptive search keeps re-checking the stalest ground once everything has been seen, and the sweep line keeps going back and forth until all 10 are reported.

Strategy A (formation sweep, report only): 12 drones abreast (columns 1, 3, ..., 23; 80 m apart) sweep from top to bottom, linked to the sentinel by a trunk of 10 relays that trail behind the line. Every POI is reported the moment it is seen, nobody stays on it, and the mission ends when all 10 are reported. The explorer count doesn't apply in this mode.

Global memory: the swarm shares one map of every cell that has ever been seen. Explorers always head for the nearest unseen cell (growing the relay network toward unseen ground when needed) and only re-check old ground once nothing is left unseen; they retarget instantly if another drone covers their cell first.

A 25×25 open field (625 cells) hides 10 targets, each placed at least 2 squares from every other target. Pick a starting swarm size with the "Drones" slider, then start a new layout. One sentinel drone posts just inside the 3-cell gate at the top-middle; every other drone enters through that gate. All drones share one staleness map: every cell counts the ticks since any drone last saw it, and each explorer heads for the cell with the highest staleness ÷ (distance + 1) that no other explorer has claimed, within a frontier that stays close to the connected network so the swarm expands outward like a growing hull. Every 5 ticks the simulation takes the convex hull of the searchers, builds a minimum spanning tree from the sentinel to those hull drones, and places relays only where an MST edge is longer than 100 m. Existing relays are reassigned to the new slots first; fresh drones are recruited only for a genuine shortfall, and surplus relays are redeployed as explorers (or sent home once everything is found). Relays and explorers are interchangeable: a relay that spots a target breaks off to claim it. A drone may briefly lose its link to the sentinel while exploring, but after 3 ticks cut off it turns back to reconnect, and after 25 it is recalled. A drone caught flip-flopping between two cells is also recalled and the network is recalculated immediately. No two drones ever share a cell. Each drone detects targets in the 1-square area around it. The mission ends once every target is found and settled.
