PUSHPAK — T sweep, report only

1000 m × 1000 m area as 25×25 cells of 40 m; one tick = one cell move at 5 m/s = 8 s. The operational centre (OPS) is 75 m left of the area, level with its middle. Every UAV takes off from OPS, flies the 75 m in (2 ticks) through the arrival lane (edge cell above the sentinel) and leaves through the departure lane (edge cell below it). The sentinel holds the middle cell of the left edge, 95 m from OPS, the only cell within the 100 m radio range, so every report passes through it.

A line of 12 drones (rows 1, 3, …, 23; 80 m apart) sweeps right and back; a trunk of 11 relays along the middle row links it to the sentinel. 10 POIs appear at random places within the first 10 minutes and must be reported within 10 s. Max flight time 20 min: each post's drone is relieved by a fresh drone launched early enough to take over before it must fly home. Drones never fly again after landing. Only drones linked to OPS act as sensors (reliefs in transit and drones flying home don't search). The swarm is recalled once all 10 POIs are reported, or at the latest time that still lets everyone land by 45:00.
